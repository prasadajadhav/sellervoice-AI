04 / UNDERSTAND

The model is one part of the product

Learned classification

Multinomial Naive Bayes learns word frequencies from 60 labelled synthetic examples across five themes. Bag-of-words features and Laplace smoothing power category scores.

Human decisions

Category corrections, severity and effort are explicit human inputs. Priorities and experiment suggestions are transparent rules and authored hypotheses, not generated insights.

Honest evaluation

Small synthetic evaluation only. Sample dashboard entries overlap training examples and must not be treated as evaluation evidence.

View evaluation and model limitations
English only; one theme per entry; no negation handling or multilingual support. Scores are uncalibrated. A top score below 60% or no recognised words triggers review. The threshold is illustrative. Multi-issue, unfamiliar or adversarial inputs may receive incorrect, high scores. Data coverage is too small for production use.
Read the product brief and validation plan
User: A marketplace product manager reviewing seller feedback. Job: Find recurring friction and choose an investigation with traceable evidence.
MVP: Batch text input → ML categorisation → human correction → frequency/severity/effort ranking → test hypothesis → CSV export. No inbox integration, automatic outreach or seller-level inference.
Proposed validation: Interview five PMs or seller-operations staff; compare manual triage with assisted triage on a consented, independently labelled dataset; measure time to triage, correction rate and per-theme precision/recall. Split data by seller/thread and include Hindi/Hinglish before expanding scope. These studies have not been performed.
Success criteria to test: Faster review without reduced classification quality, fewer ignored severe issues, and useful evidence for prioritisation. Downstream business outcomes require separate controlled experiments; no lift is claimed.
