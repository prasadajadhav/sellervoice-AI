What makes this machine learning?

The model learns word frequencies from 48 labelled synthetic emails. It uses bag-of-words text features and multinomial Naive Bayes with Laplace smoothing to rank four categories. Routing suggestions are fixed rules after classification.

Inspect the held-out evaluation

16 separately written synthetic examples, four per category. These examples are not used for training. This small illustrative set cannot establish real-world accuracy.

Limitations and next steps
English only; small synthetic dataset; one category per email. Mixed requests, negation, unfamiliar language and domain changes may be misclassified. Unknown vocabulary or a top score below 60% produces “Needs review”. Next steps: collect permissioned real examples, split by account/thread, compare a TF-IDF baseline, calibrate scores and assess per-category precision/recall.
Independent learning prototype developed with AI assistance. No employer data. No production deployment or business-impact claims.
