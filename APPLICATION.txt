Project: SellerVoice AI – Seller Feedback Prioritisation.
Developed an AI-assisted prototype for a B2B marketplace product-management workflow. It classifies seller feedback into five issue themes, enables human review and label correction, and prioritises investigation using feedback frequency, severity and estimated effort. The interactive dashboard includes segment filters, batch feedback input, evidence review, editable priorities, experiment hypotheses and CSV export. Technologies: JavaScript, NLP, bag-of-words features, multinomial Naive Bayes with Laplace smoothing, HTML/CSS, ChatGPT-assisted development and GitHub Pages. Uses synthetic data; priority scoring is rule-based and the prototype has not been validated on actual marketplace data.
Demo: https://prasadajadhav.github.io/account-information-tool/
Repository: https://github.com/prasadajadhav/account-information-tool

30-SECOND EXPLANATION
I built SellerVoice AI with AI coding assistance to explore how a marketplace PM could turn seller feedback into product decisions. Naive Bayes groups feedback into themes; a human can correct those labels. An explicit frequency, severity and effort formula supports prioritisation, with evidence and a proposed experiment. The prototype uses synthetic data, so I would validate it with actual users and permissioned feedback before making business claims.

WHY NAIVE BAYES?
Small, explainable and fast; runs locally without API costs. It is a baseline and struggles with context, negation and multilingual inputs. A larger dataset, comparison models and independent evaluation are necessary.

WHAT DOES 95% MEAN?
19 of 20 synthetic test examples were correctly classified; one was referred for review. This tiny synthetic test is not real-world accuracy. Do not present it as a validated business result.
